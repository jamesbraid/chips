# SGI ASIC coverage audit

A coverage audit of the SGI-designed ASICs and option-board chips in this repository, dated 2026-11-15 and grouped by machine family.

Grades:

- **full**: a register map with fields, plus a reviewed spec. No row reaches this grade.
- **partial**: a register map or page exists, with gaps in fields, reset values or coverage.
- **stub**: a page or contract with little or no register content.
- **missing**: no unit, page or register map in this repository.
- **elsewhere**: owned by another repository, named in the row.
- **unsure**: the audit could not settle whether the part exists or what it is.
- **out of scope**: not a MIPS or IA-64 part.

## Most important gaps

- The Indy and Indigo2 core has no page: MC, INT2 and INT3, the HPC3 SCSI and PBUS channels, HAL2 and VINO.
- The Challenge and Onyx Everest set has no page.
- The UV hub has no page, and neither do its GRU and BAU sub-blocks or NUMAlink 4 to 7.
- Hub, HEART, IOC3, Bridge, MACE and CRIME each have a large page but no fields or reset values.

## Scope of this audit

The audit read chip pages and register maps only. It ran no binary analysis and did not open the vendor source tree. Some public-source lookups were not completed, so "missing" means not found in the sources searched. A row's grade is the auditors' judgement, not a review. A few units still appear in two families with the same or matching grades (VICE, IOC4, Crossbow, the Bridge and XBridge pair, Newport and the PCI board rows); they were not merged in this pass.
