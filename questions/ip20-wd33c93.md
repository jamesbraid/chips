# Indigo WD33C93: what the SCSI controller page leaves open

Question. Where do the held sources stop for the WD33C93-family controller on the Indigo? The settled parts are on
[the WD33C93 page](../devices/wd33c93/README.md).

Status: **unknown** for everything below.

- **Which chip revision the R4000 board fits.** IRIX and the 4.0.5D PROM only distinguish status `0x00` from `0x01` after Reset, and
  read index `0x03` as a revision byte. A public NetBSD capture of an R3000 Indigo shows a WD33C93A, revision byte 0. Route: a boot
  capture from an R4000 Indigo or a board photograph of the part marking; the IRIX `hinv` output does not name it.
- **The board's clock frequency.** IRIX and the PROM write clock select 2 (16 to 20 MHz) and a selection timeout of `0x40`, which
  is 256 ms at 20 MHz and 320 ms at 16 MHz. No shipped code names the crystal. Route: the Indigo schematic, or a timing measurement.
- **What the revision byte on a WD33C93B reads.** IRIX treats 11 and above as chip type 8 and anything lower as type 4; the data
  sheet does not state a revision code. Route: the WD33C93B data sheet, not held.
- **Whether the HPC1 SCSI control bit 0 pulse also asserts SCSI RST on the bus.** NetBSD says it resets "the channel and the external
  controller"; IRIX calls the routine `wd93_resetbus`. The effect on the chip (status zero, INT set) is read from NetBSD's
  probe only. Route: the HPC1 specification, or the HPC1 page when it lands.
- **How the HPC1 gates and latches the chip's interrupt request into INT2 local 0 bit 2.** Route: the HPC1 page.
- **Byte access under little-endian CPU mode and with halfword or word accesses.** Every shipped access is a byte at the big-endian
  lane `+2`; what the HPC1 returns for a word read (NetBSD's probe reads a word and shifts) is not in the IRIX or PROM code. Route:
  the HPC1 page.
- **The PROM 4.0.5G SCSI code.** The image is not held on this host. The [PROM page](../platforms/ip20/prom-software.md#405g-differences)
  says its SCSI driver was not diffed against 4.0.5D. Route: put the image in `root/` and repeat the reset-sequence reading.
- **What the driver flag bytes `0x60` and the per-controller word mean.** The `scuzzy` table's byte at offset `0x20` (`0x60`) is
  ORed with the chip type into the driver's flags byte, whose bits 5 and 6 are not decoded here. Route: the rest of `wd93.o`
  (the abort and subrelease routines).
- **The Abort and Assert ATN call sites.** The command codes `0x01` and `0x02` are inferred from delay-slot arguments to
  the retry routine, not from a direct store.
